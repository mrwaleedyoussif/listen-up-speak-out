Privacy

From Zero To Hero (English Language) · Andalus International Schools – Taif Boys · Designed by Mr. Waleed Youssef

What we collect

When you press Sign in with Google, Google tells the app your name and e-mail address. The app uses them only to open your account and show your name. We do not ask Google for any other information, and we never see your Google password.

Where your information is kept

Your account, lesson progress, scores and voice recordings are saved only on the device you use (in the browser's storage). The app has no server and does not upload, sell or share your information. Clearing the browser's data removes it.

Who can sign in

Students sign in with a Gmail address or an Andalus school e-mail address (@as.edu.sa). The teacher's account is the owner's Google account.
