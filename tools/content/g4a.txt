For /d/ (day), your tongue touches the place behind your top teeth. | لنطق /ð/ كما في they ضع لسانك بين أسنانك مع إخراج الصوت مثل حرف الذال، أما /d/ كما في day فيلمس اللسان ما خلف الأسنان العليا.

pair: then | den
pair: those | doze

tip: Prices are big numbers. Listen to both parts: thirty-five = 35, forty-two = 42. | الأسعار أعداد كبيرة. استمع إلى الجزأين: thirty-five تعني 35، وforty-two تعني 42.
script:
NOOR: Sami, we have fifty riyals. Let's buy a present for Fahd.

NOOR: That's a lot. What about the teddy bear?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: The teddy bear is forty-two riyals.
SAMI: Hmm. And the puzzle with the animals?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: It's only eighteen riyals. It's for children aged two to four.
NOOR: Fahd is two now. That's perfect!
SAMI: Can we have the puzzle and a small ball too?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: The small ball is twelve riyals. So that's thirty riyals, please.
NOOR: Here's fifty riyals.
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: Thank you. Here's your change: twenty riyals.
end

tf: The puzzle is for children aged two to four. | T

gap: The small ball costs ___ riyals. | twelve, 12

speak: Play shop with your partner. Ask about the prices of three things. | How much is the ___? — It's ___ riyals.
teach: Prepare paper riyal notes. After listening, students "buy" toys from the board and calculate the change in pairs (RallyCoach). Noor's "That's a lot" is a polite way to say something is expensive — teach it as a chunk.

@SPEAK 1 | Fifteen or Fifty? | Asking about prices | السؤال عن الأسعار
kagan: Quiz-Quiz-Trade | RallyCoach
model:
NOOR: Excuse me. How much is this notebook?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: It's fifteen riyals.
NOOR: Fifteen or fifty?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: Fifteen. One, five.
NOOR: Oh, good! And how much are these colored pens?
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: They're thirteen riyals for the small box and thirty riyals for the big box.
NOOR: I'd like the small box, please.
WOMAN[Shop Assistant]: So that's twenty-eight riyals, please.
NOOR: Here you are. Thank you!
end
roles: NOOR | WOMAN
chunks: How much is this ___? = بكم هذا ___؟ | How much are these? = بكم هذه؟ | It's fifteen riyals. = إنه بخمسة عشر ريالًا. | Fifteen or fifty? = خمسة عشر أم خمسون؟ | I'd like the small box. = أريد العلبة الصغيرة.
sound: pairs | Thirteen or thirty? | In -teen numbers the strong beat is at the end: thir-TEEN. In -ty numbers it is at the start: THIR-ty. Listen for the long "teen" and the final /n/. | في الأعداد المنتهية بـ -teen يكون المقطع القوي في النهاية thir-TEEN، وفي الأعداد المنتهية بـ -ty يكون في البداية THIR-ty. استمع لـ teen الطويلة ولصوت /n/ في آخرها.
pair: thirteen | thirty
pair: fourteen | forty
pair: fifteen | fifty
pair: sixteen | sixty
pair: eighteen | eighty
task: Make price cards for five things. Your partner asks and writes the prices. Then check together. | How much is the ___? — It's ___ riyals. — ___ or ___? — ___!
cando: I can ask about prices. | I can check a number I'm not sure about. | I can hear -teen and -ty numbers.
teach: Students tap the strong beat on the desk: tap-TAP for thirTEEN, TAP-tap for THIRty. Checking ("Fifteen or fifty?") is a real-life skill — praise students who use it.
