@LISTEN 1 | Desert Life | Listening for facts and reasons | الاستماع إلى الحقائق والأسباب
kagan: Numbered Heads Together | RallyRobin

script:
NARRATOR: The Arabian Desert is one of the hottest places on Earth. In summer, the sand can be hotter than seventy degrees! But many animals live here. How do they do it?
NARRATOR: The Arabian oryx is a beautiful white animal with long, straight horns. Its white coat reflects the sun, so it stays cooler than darker animals. The oryx can live for weeks without drinking. It gets water from the plants it eats.
NARRATOR: The fennec fox is smaller than a cat, but its ears are bigger than its face! Its big ears help it to lose heat. It sleeps underground in the day, where it's cooler, and it hunts at night.
NARRATOR: The camel is the most famous desert animal. It can drink more than one hundred liters of water in ten minutes. Its long eyelashes protect its eyes from the sand.
NARRATOR: In {1972|nineteen seventy-two}, the Arabian oryx almost disappeared from the wild. Today, thanks to protection programs, there are more than a thousand oryx in the wild again.
end

@SPEAK 1 | Which One Is Better? | Comparing things | مقارنة الأشياء
